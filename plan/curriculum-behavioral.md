# Behavioural & Interview-Craft Curriculum — Googleyness, Stories, the Loop Itself

**Weeks 57–66, with story drafting seeded earlier.** Google's loop scores four things: General
Cognitive Ability, Role-Related Knowledge, Leadership, and Googleyness. The last two are the
behavioural round — and it can sink a strong coding packet. The interview-craft topics (IC-)
cover what the coding rounds grade beyond correctness: communication, testing your own code,
writing in a doc without an IDE.

The ten story slots are listed in `trackers/behavioral.md` — fill each with a story from your
own work. Stories live in `notes/behavioral/stories/`.

## Story template (`notes/behavioral/stories/NN-slug.md`)

```markdown
# Story NN — <one-line title>
Slots: <which of the 10>   Googleyness: <which traits>
## Situation (15 s)
## Task (15 s)
## Action (60–75 s) — "I", specific decisions, the trade-off you weighed
## Result (20 s) — a number, then what you learned
## Follow-ups I expect, and my answers
```

Spoken length 90–120 s. Record yourself; if it runs over 2:15, cut the situation.
