## Slot Definitions

### Morning slot (Mon–Fri, 08:30 – 10:00, 1.5h)
**Purpose:** Pure DSA coding. Fresh brain → highest leverage.
**Do:**
- Re-attempt yesterday's failed problems (10 min)
- Solve today's problem quota (45-60 min)
- Capture notes inline (15 min)
**Don't:**
- Read theory
- Watch videos
- Update trackers

### Evening slot (Mon–Fri, 20:00 – 21:30, 1.5h)
**Purpose:** Theory, notes, video learning. Tired brain → passive intake is fine.
**Do:**
- Watch a topic video (30-45 min)
- Take structured notes in `notes/`
- Read core subjects chapters
- Refine a STAR story (15 min, optional)
**Don't:**
- Try hard DSA problems (you'll burn out)
- Multi-task with social media

### Saturday block (09:00 – 13:00, 4h)
**Purpose:** Deep work. Hardest problems. Long-form HLD walkthroughs. Mock interview (in Phases 3-5).
**Structure:**
- 09:00 – 10:30 — Hard problems (1 hard or 2 hard mediums) + redo failures from the week
- 10:30 – 11:00 — Break, snack
- 11:00 – 12:00 — HLD/LLD deep dive (Phase 3+) OR core subjects intensive (Phase 1-2)
- 12:00 – 13:00 — **Mock interview** (Phase 3+) OR weekly review prep (compile data for Sunday writing)

### Sunday — Rest + 30 min review
**Purpose:** Recovery. Plan next week.
**Do (Sun morning, ~30-45 min):**
- Write weekly review (`reviews/weekly/YYYY-MM-DD-to-YYYY-MM-DD.md`)
- Update all 8 trackers
- Set focus for next week
**Don't:**
- Solve new problems
- Open LeetCode
- Worry about catching up

## Phase-Specific Adjustments

### Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4) — Foundations
- Mornings: heavier on STL + complexity refresh, lighter problem count
- Evenings: theory + OS chapters
- Saturday: longer revision, fewer hard problems

### Phase 2 (Weeks 5-12) — DSA Patterns
- Mornings: 2-3 medium problems
- Evenings: pattern theory + OS/DBMS/CN
- Saturday: 1 hard problem + revision of week

### Phase 3 (Weeks 13-17) — Advanced + LLD
- Morning routine shifts to LLD theory + diagrams during Weeks 15-17 (less coding, more designing)
- Saturday afternoon: mock interview (Week 15+)

### Phase 4 (Weeks 18-22) — HLD
- Mornings: HLD case study walkthroughs
- Evenings: deep-dive companion topics
- Saturday: mock interview + revise prior week's design

### Phase 5 (Weeks 23-26) — Polish
- Mornings: behavioral story refinement, weak-area drills
- Evenings: rotation review (DSA / HLD / LLD)
- Saturday: full-loop sim Week 25, otherwise final mocks

## When Life Happens

**One-off disruption (sick day, family emergency):**
- Log it as "skipped" in the daily log
- Don't double-up the next day
- Move on

**Travel for work:**
- Drop evening sessions if hotel internet bad
- Morning sessions usually still doable (laptop + LC)
- Aim for 50% normal volume during travel weeks
- Log honestly

**Heavy work weeks (release crunches, on-call):**
- Pre-week: tell the coach "this week will be 30% capacity"
- Stick to morning slots only
- Skip Saturday block guilt-free
- Resume normal next week

**Burnout signals (act on, don't push through):**
- 5+ days of energy/focus <6/10 in daily logs
- Skipping evening sessions repeatedly
- Dreading the morning slot

If you see these, **take a full week off**, don't try to muscle through. The phase extends 1 week. Better than crashing the whole plan.

## Calendar Recurring Blocks (set these once)

In Google Calendar / Outlook / whatever, create:

1. **Daily 08:30 – 10:00** — "Study: DSA AM" — Mon-Fri
2. **Daily 20:00 – 21:30** — "Study: Theory PM" — Mon-Fri
3. **Saturday 09:00 – 13:00** — "Study: Deep Block"
4. **Sunday 09:30 – 10:15** — "Study: Weekly Review"
5. **Last Sunday of month 09:00 – 10:30** — "Study: Monthly Review" (replaces weekly review that day)

Set notifications. Treat them as meetings. Don't reschedule lightly.

## What This Schedule Optimizes For

- **Sustainability** — 19h/week is real-world feasible alongside a senior engineer's day job
- **Cognitive matching** — hard cognitive work in morning, easier intake in evening
- **One full rest day** — Sunday recovery is non-negotiable; you'll perform worse without it
- **One deep-work block** — Saturday allows the kind of deep DSA / HLD work that fragmented sessions can't deliver
- **Spaced repetition built-in** — weekly + monthly reviews force you to re-touch material
