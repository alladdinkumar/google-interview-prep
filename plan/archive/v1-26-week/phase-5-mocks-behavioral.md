## Weekly Breakdown

### Week 23 — STAR Stories + Weak DSA Revision
**Hours: 19** | **🎯 Mock #9 (Behavioral)**

Behavioral prep takes more time than people think. Don't shortcut it — Google's Googleyness round and Amazon's LP rounds *eliminate* candidates who breeze through DSA.

- AM Mon: Read your resume + brainstorm 12-15 raw incidents (scaling EDR sim, debugging telemetry pipeline, mentoring junior, conflict with PM, infra outage, etc.). Pick top 10 to develop.
- AM Tue: Refine 3 stories using STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result + Learning). Time each to ≤2 min.
- AM Wed: Refine next 3 stories. Map each to ≥2 leadership principles (Amazon) + Googleyness traits.
- AM Thu: Refine remaining 4 stories.
- AM Fri: Rehearse out loud (record yourself). Listen back. Tighten wording.
- PM Mon-Wed: Weak DSA topics revision (pick top 3 from `trackers/weak-areas.md`)
- PM Thu-Fri: Re-solve 6 medium problems on those weak topics
- Sat: **Mock #9** (behavioral-only, with a peer or DSA-coach.com)
- Sun: weekly review

Files: `notes/behavioral/stories/01-edr-scaling.md`, `02-telemetry-debug.md`, ... `10-mentorship.md`. Master index in `notes/behavioral/_stories-index.md`.

### Week 25 — Full-Loop Simulation + Revision
**Hours: 19** | **🎯 Mock #11 (HLD)** | **📅 Sat = full-day loop**

- AM Mon-Fri: targeted revision — pick the lowest-scoring sub-area from the last 5 mocks, drill it
- PM Mon-Fri: rehearse STAR stories daily (rotate)

**Saturday: Full-loop simulation** — 5 back-to-back rounds, 45 min each, 15-min breaks. Use Pramp or schedule a buddy.
1. Coding round 1 (medium DSA, 45 min)
2. Coding round 2 (medium-hard DSA, 45 min)

4. LLD (45 min)

Total: ~5h. This is your dress rehearsal. Log everything.

- Sun: detailed retrospective on the full loop. **Be honest about what fell apart under fatigue.** Plan Week 26 around fixing it.

## Application Strategy

- **Direct apply** (careers page) — fastest signal
- **Referrals** — collect via LinkedIn during Phase 4; ask once you're past Week 23
- **Recruiters** — accept LinkedIn recruiter messages but don't chase
- **Don't apply to your top 1-2 dream companies first.** Apply to "tier 2" first (Microsoft, Atlassian) to use as warm-up real interviews. Save Google/Meta for after you've had 1-2 real screens.

## What "Done" Looks Like on 2026-11-15

You can honestly tell yourself:

> "I've solved 290 LeetCode problems. I can drive a fresh HLD interview through capacity → API → DB → scale without thinking. I have 10 STAR stories I can deliver in 90-120 seconds each. I've done 12 mocks and the last 3 averaged 7/10. I have 3+ phone screens lined up. If I get rejected, it's because they got a better candidate — not because I wasn't ready."

If you can't say that, **don't apply to the top dream company yet.** Push the timeline 2-3 weeks and finish the gap.
