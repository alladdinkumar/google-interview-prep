# FAQ

---

### Q: I missed a few days. Should I delete those daily logs?

**A: No.** Leave them. If you didn't study, create the file anyway with "skipped — reason: __" in the Reflection section. **Skipped days are data.** Patterns of skipping reveal more than streak counts ever do.

---

### Q: Should I do 4 hours on Sunday instead of resting?

**A: No.** Sunday rest is non-negotiable. Sleep researchers (and Olympic athletes) agree: recovery is when adaptation happens. You'll perform worse on Monday morning if you grind Sunday.

---

### Q: I want to add Topic X to the curriculum. How?

**A: Don't, unless monthly review says so.** Adding topics mid-phase is a procrastination signal. Write the request in your weekly review's "Plan adjustments" section. The coach will evaluate at month-end.

---

### Q: I'm ahead of schedule. Can I move to the next phase early?

**A: Be cautious.** Look at the *exit criteria* of your current phase. If you can honestly check all boxes, you can transition. If you're just bored, you're not actually done — you have weak spots you haven't tested. Use the extra time to drill weak areas or revise old topics.

---

### Q: I'm behind. Should I extend the phase?

**A: Yes — by default.** Compressing the next phase is how plans collapse. Extending by 1 week pushes target date by 1 week. That's fine — better to be 1 week late than to skip foundational topics.

If you're behind 2+ weeks total, do a monthly re-scope: push target date by 2-3 weeks. Don't try to cram.

---

### Q: I solved a problem but used a hint. Does it count?

**A: Track it honestly.** Log it as "needed hint." Adding it to your daily count but flagging the hint is correct. The point is to surface weakness in `trackers/weak-areas.md`, not to game your own metrics.

---

### Q: I keep watching videos but not solving problems. How do I stop?

**A: Hard rule: 2-problems-per-video.** Watch a 30-min video → solve 2 problems on that topic before opening another video. If you don't solve the 2, no more videos that day. Videos are *passive*; solving is *active*. Active learning sticks.

---

### Q: Should I use a paid resource (LC Premium, Educative)?

**A: Only after free-tier ceiling.** Most candidates over-invest in paid resources. Free Striver + NeetCode + GFG + Gaurav Sen + ByteByteGo is enough for a Google offer. If by Week 12 you've genuinely consumed the free material end-to-end and need company-specific filters, then LC Premium ($35/mo for 1 month before applications) is the highest-ROI purchase.

---

### Q: When should I tell my manager I'm interviewing elsewhere?

**A: Not until you have an offer.** Privafy doesn't need to know. Don't even hint. Manage references carefully — use former colleagues outside Privafy or peers (not your manager) as references unless asked otherwise.

---

### Q: What if I get an offer mid-prep (before Phase 5)?

**A: Take the call seriously.** Offers don't wait for plans. If a real opportunity comes, you can:
- Accelerate Phase 5 (compress mocks to 2/week)
- Skip remaining HLD case studies you haven't done; focus on the ones the company asks
- Be honest with yourself about readiness — don't say yes to an interview you'd fail

The plan is for *being ready*, not for *adhering to a calendar*.

---

### Q: Can I switch from C++ to Python mid-prep?

**A: Strongly discouraged.** Switching language mid-prep loses ~3-4 weeks of fluency. The "right" language is the one you're most fluent in *by Week 20*, not the "best" one in theory. Stick with C++.

The exception: if you find yourself slower in C++ than in Python after Week 4 of practice, that's a signal. Re-evaluate on the 1-week mark of Phase 2.

---

### Q: How do I do mocks without revealing I'm interviewing?

**A: Free peer mocks (Pramp, reddit study buddies) don't require disclosure.** You're just two strangers practicing. Paid mocks (interviewing.io with real FAANG engineers) are anonymous to your employer. Don't worry about it.

---

### Q: My daily log is getting tedious. Can I skip sections?

**A: Skip Behavioral Prep section freely (Phase 5 only).** Don't skip:
- YAML frontmatter (machines parse this)
- Problems Solved entries (data)
- Reflection section (your future self needs this)

If the template feels long, that's a sign to streamline the template, not skip filling it. Bring it up in monthly review.

---

### Q: What if Claude Code is unavailable?

**A: This system works without Claude.** Everything is plain markdown. You can do weekly reviews by hand (open the 6 logs, fill in the template). Claude accelerates the busywork; it doesn't enable the system.

---

### Q: How do I handle the moment of "I don't want to do this today"?

**A: Show up anyway. Cut the session in half.** 90 min today is better than 0 min today and "I'll do 180 tomorrow" (you won't). The friction of starting is the highest barrier. Once you open the daily log and write the first line, momentum usually arrives. If 30 min in you genuinely have nothing left, stop and log honestly.

The rule: **negotiate down, not down to zero.**

---

### Q: My partner / family is unhappy with the time commitment.

**A: This is a real concern.** Re-read your time budget:
- 1.5h before work (you'd be commuting / scrolling otherwise)
- 1.5h after work (yes, this is family time)
- 4h Saturday (yes, this is weekend time)

Conversations to have:
- "This is finite (26 weeks)" — give a date
- "I'll keep [X commitment to family]" — explicit carve-out
- "I need [Y support from you]" — specific ask

Don't sneak prep time. It corrodes relationships and your own focus. Be upfront, get buy-in, deliver.

If you genuinely can't get the time commitment, **reduce the plan**, don't compromise both family and prep. 12 hours/week × 9 months > 19 hours/week of resentment.

---

### Q: What if I fail an interview?

**A: That's data.** Rejection ≠ inadequacy. Big companies reject ~95% of candidates. Each interview gives you signal on weak spots. After a rejection:

1. Log everything you can remember in `notes/post-interview/<company>-<date>.md`
2. Identify top 3 things that fell apart
3. Add to `trackers/weak-areas.md`
4. Don't take 2 weeks off to "process" — re-engage within 3 days
5. Apply to a different company within 1 week

Most engineers who get FAANG offers had 1-3 prior rejections. It's normal.

### Q: When can I delete this whole `Study/` folder?

**A: After you've been at the new role for 6 months.** Keep it as reference if you want to coach someone else. Otherwise: archive (zip + cloud), delete locally if it bothers you.
